How it works

Designed for browser constraints.

Coarse-grained parallelism avoids shared-memory deployment requirements while keeping the workflow understandable.

1

Split on keyframes

A planner remuxes the source into independently decodable Matroska segments.

2

Encode in parallel

Each worker owns an isolated single-thread WebAssembly heap and processes one segment at a time.

3

Reassemble in order

The farm concatenates the encoded segments and muxes one continuous audio track.

Works best for

  • H.264, VP9, resizing and pixel-format conversion
  • Independent filters without long temporal state
  • Medium and long clips with several keyframes

Know the limits

  • Every worker owns a full FFmpeg heap
  • Two-pass encoding cannot be split transparently
  • Tiny files may be faster with a single worker
